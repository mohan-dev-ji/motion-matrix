# Episode 001 — Halves

## Topic

Recognising half of a shape. Starting with simple examples, then revealing how many ways you can actually shade half of a shape — more than most people expect.

## Hook (0:00 - 0:08)

"How many ways can you shade half of a shape? You might think the answer is obvious... but it's not."

Visual: A hexagon appears, cleanly split down the middle. One half shades in. Simple, familiar. Then a quick flash — all 20 possible combinations flicker across the screen. Pause.

## Scene 1: The Circle (0:08 - 0:35)

"Let's start simple. Here's a circle, cut into four equal pieces."

Visual: Circle divides into 4 equal slices.

"To shade half, we need two out of four."

Visual: Two adjacent slices shade in.

"That's one way. But what about this?"

Visual: Reset. Two opposite slices shade in.

"Or this?"

Visual: Cycle through remaining combinations — there are C(4,2) = 6 total.

"There are actually six different ways to shade exactly half of this circle."

Visual: All 6 combinations displayed in a small grid.

## Scene 2: The Hexagon (0:35 - 1:25)

"Now let's try something bigger. A hexagon, cut into six equal pieces."

Visual: Hexagon appears, divides into 6 triangular slices.

"To shade half, we need three out of six."

Visual: The obvious symmetric half shades in (3 adjacent slices).

"That's the obvious one. But how many ways are there in total?"

Visual: Start cycling through combinations — slowly at first (3-4 examples), then accelerating.

"Twenty. There are twenty different ways to shade exactly half of this hexagon."

Visual: All 20 combinations displayed in a 4x5 grid. Hold for a beat.

## Scene 3: The Pattern (1:25 - 2:00)

"So what's happening here? With four slices, we had six ways. With six slices, twenty."

Visual: Side by side — circle (4 slices, 6 ways) and hexagon (6 slices, 20 ways).

"The more pieces you cut a shape into, the more ways there are to find half."

Visual: Briefly flash an octagon (8 slices) — C(8,4) = 70 combinations ripple across it rapidly. Don't explain the formula, just let the visual land.

"And that's just halves."

Visual: Hold on the octagon, then fade to black.

## End Card (2:00 - 2:15)

"Next time — what happens when we look for thirds?"

Visual: A circle divided into 6, with 2 slices shaded (one third). Motion Matrix logo. Subscribe prompt.
